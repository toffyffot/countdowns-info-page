Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 3, 2026

Overview

Countdowns is designed to keep your data private. We do not collect or sell personal information.

Data We Collect

  • Local app data: Countdown titles, dates, tags, colors, and notes are stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud (optional): If you enable iCloud Sync, your countdown data is stored in your private iCloud account and synced across your devices.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions are handled by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device. Notification data does not leave your device.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

Your Choices

  • You can disable iCloud Sync in Settings.
  • You can delete countdowns at any time.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local data.
